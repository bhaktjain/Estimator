**Project:** 366 Broadway Apt PH-A (11th & 12th Floor Duplex)  
**Source:** Construction Documents (CDs) – 01.20.26  
**Estimate Date:** February 2026

---

## Scope Summary (from CDs)

### 12th Floor
- **Kitchen:** Full gut and renovation
  - New cabinets (base + upper), island
  - Quartz countertops and backsplash
  - New appliances (refrigerator, range/oven, dishwasher, microwave)
- **Flooring:** New wood flooring throughout entire 12th floor (~1,300 SF)
- **Stair:** Remodel of convenience stair to 11th floor (extends to roof bulkhead)
  - New 3' wide stair structure: 19 risers @ 8" high, 18 treads @ 11" deep
  - New handrail and balusters

### 11th Floor
- **Primary Bathroom (~89 SF):**
  - Free-standing tub, toilet, shower (steam), double vanity
  - Full tile (floor and walls), waterproofing
  - Glass shower enclosure
- **Shared Bathroom (~58 SF):** Full renovation
- **In-Suite Bathroom (~65 SF):** Full renovation
- **Primary Bedroom:** New walk-in closet
- **Bedrooms #1, #2, #3:** New millwork closet interiors
- **Laundry:** Relocation to new location
- **Coffee Station:** Built-in off hallway with undercounter fridge
- **Radiator Covers:** New painted wood covers (7 locations per plan)
- **Interior Doors:** New doors per schedule (11 standard + 2 pocket)
