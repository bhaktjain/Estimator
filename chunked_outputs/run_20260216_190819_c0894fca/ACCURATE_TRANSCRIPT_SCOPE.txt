PROJECT CONTEXT
---------------
• Mother's house; owner (daughter) and husband (Cory) plan to move from Texas.
• Goal: expand to the back so family can live there; mother stays (40 years, widow, medical in area).
• Location: Miami Gardens.
• Contractor (Eran, Mo) transcribing; detailed proposal with permits.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. EXPANSION / NEW CONSTRUCTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Back extension: full length and full width of house — "50 by 20" feet (~1,000 SF addition).
• Garage: phase 2 option; two-car, closed; ~18' x 25–30' (butt up to driveway).
• Laundry: move from current location; fit in expansion (e.g. near garage/water heater area).
• New spaces in addition:
  - Mother's master: expanded bedroom + hallway + walk-in closet + new bathroom (shower only).
  - Second full bathroom (for couple) — back-to-back with mother's for plumbing.
  - Second bedroom with closet (for couple).
  - Possible: sitting area, small office (husband works from home).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. DEMOLITION / REMOVAL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Pergola outside: "that would be taken down" — part of project.
• Balcony/terrace: "that balcony or terrace it's going to become a part of the house."
• Sliding glass door: "this lighting glass door would be gone" (transition/opening TBD).
• Kitchen: full gut — everything brand new (cabinets, stone, tile floors).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. KITCHEN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Expand open-concept; push out ~3 ft (and ~1 ft other way); beam (load-bearing wall).
• New: dishwasher, double sink, more cabinetry, open to living.
• Everything brand new: cabinets, stone countertops, tile on floors.
• Style: black and white kitchen — black granite (or black with dots) countertops, white cabinets.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. FLOORING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Whole house new floor — "assume you want to do the whole house new floor."
• Tile throughout entire house (same tile); "other than the bathroom" same floor, then tile.
• Bathrooms can be same or different tile (e.g. 12x24); shower floor anti-slip.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. MOTHER'S NEW BATHROOM (in expansion)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Brand new bathroom from scratch (shower only, no tub — she's handicapped).
• Accessibility: bars; no bench initially (can add later).
• Tile in shower only — all the way to ceiling; drywall elsewhere. Glass/sliding door.
• Niche for shampoo/soap; linen closet or storage for towels.
• One sink, good size; white or beige vanity.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. COUPLE'S BATHROOM (in expansion)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Two sinks; shower, no tub.
• Tile: 12x24 flooring/walls; shower floor hexagon or similar, anti-slip; niche.
• Wood cabinets; countertop white or black.
• Both new bathrooms back-to-back for plumbing.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. PAINTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• "Painting the whole house" — put a price for everything and a price to paint everything; client can take out.
• New construction obviously painted; "the rest of the house" — price to paint everything.
• Exterior (existing): "might have to be painted to match."
• No texture on walls — flat, smooth.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. MEP / RELOCATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• HVAC: relocate the large rear unit ("the only one inside would be that back that big one").
• Water heater: move from outside to inside — "into like the laundry space."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. OTHER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Fence: "We would redo the fence as well" — this phase or later TBD.
• Roof (addition): want pitched, not flat (water pooling concern); options to be discussed with architect/team.
• Living room: keep as living room; other space family/dining/flex.
• Existing room (in blue): keep as room for family stays; don't expand into it.
• Foundation/level: expansion will be level (no step down); sliding door gone, transition TBD.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. PHASING / EXCLUSIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Garage: phase 2; proposal can include with and without garage.
• Permits: all work with permits.
• Architect: contractor will refer licensed architect for drawings.
• Timeline: design 1–1.5 months; city can be 6 months–1 year; construction ~7–9 months (demo to finish); strategy to minimize disruption if someone living there (e.g. kitchen last).
