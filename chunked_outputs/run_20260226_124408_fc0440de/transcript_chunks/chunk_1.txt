Summary:

The meeting reviewed the renovation scope and finish selections for 475 Brickell Avenue, unit 2605. The team confirmed demolition of existing flooring throughout the apartment and decided to install interlocking wood flooring across all living areas, while all bathrooms will receive tile that mimics wood rather than real hardwood. Overall flooring scope and demo
The group confirmed demolition of existing flooring and agreed to demo the entire apartment floor area, with interlocking wood flooring planned for the main spaces. Bathrooms were explicitly excluded from wood flooring and will instead receive tile that resembles wood.
* The team will demo existing floors and install interlocking wood flooring throughout the apartment living areas

Materials, samples, and bedrooms
Participants discussed preferred materials, referencing "Brazilian Amor" as a wood selection and agreeing that Participant 2 will supply samples for review. The walkthrough included a second bedroom and the decision that bathrooms would not use wood flooring.
* Participant 2 will provide finish samples for selection after the walkthrough

Bathroom scope and fixtures
The team agreed to replace bathroom fixtures and vanities, raising vanity height and considering two sinks in the larger bath; showers were to be kept where practical. They discussed using tile that complements adjacent finishes and considered modern fixtures including electronic toilets and floating vanities.
* All bathroom fixtures and vanities will be replaced, with vanities raised and consideration for double sinks in the larger bathroom
* Showers will generally be retained rather than removed
* Bathrooms will use tile that looks like wood rather than real wood and will not receive wood flooring

Interior layout changes and doors
Participants explored building out a partial wall, raising door heights, creating a pantry from a laddered space, and preferring frameless or flat doors for a modern look. They discussed removing glass and mirrors to simplify finishes and open sightlines.
* Mirrors and glass elements identified for removal will be demoed

Kitchen condition and minor updates
The kitchen was judged to be in generally good condition with recommendations for a backsplash and possible minor countertop refresh rather than full replacement. The group reviewed space constraints for seating and discussed keeping the existing kitchen footprint to avoid crowding the dining area.
* Kitchen work will be limited to a backsplash and countertop refresh to avoid extensive demolition

Finishes, aesthetics, and final preferences
The conversation concluded with aesthetic choices such as wallpaper or a strong focal bed wall, personalized wallpaper options, marble-like finishes, and floating cabinetry for a contemporary look. The team reiterated removal of bidets and selection of fixtures and colors that are not overly dark.
* The bidet will be removed from at least one bathroom during renovation
