Terms & Conditions
Design
Chapter Design provides a comprehensive service for Design and Material Specification.
Design services include: Finishes (built in millwork, tile, stone, flooring, paint), Fixtures (plumbing, appliances, glazing,
recessed lighting) and Additional Elements (Doors, hardware, moldings, trims, sills).
Design services exclude furniture and furnishings, window treatments, wallpaper, and decorative lighting.
Specification Phase: Material selections will be presented during the specification phase. A 15-hour design allowance is
included.
Design Phase: Deliverables include floor plans, wall elevations, five (5) final photorealistic renderings and one (1) final
set of Construction Drawings
Revisions Policy: Up to three (3) rounds of design revisions are included. Additional revisions beyond this limit will be
billed at $250 per hour. Revisions requested after final design sign-off will also incur hourly billing at $250 per hour.
On-site Design Meetings: The project includes five (5) in-person design meetings. Any additional meetings or work
beyond the original scope will be billed at $250 per hour.
Warranty
Hello Chapter provides a three-year warranty from the project completion date, covering workmanship and installation
defects directly resulting from our services.
This warranty applies only to deficiencies caused by errors in Chapter's work and does not cover:
Normal wear and tear, misuse, or improper maintenance.
Damage caused by external factors, such as water leaks, structural movement, or acts of nature.
Materials and products, whether procured by Chapter or the client, as these are covered solely by the manufacturer’s
warranty.
Procurement
Hello Chapter will handle the procurement, ordering, shipping, and coordination of all finish materials and fixtures for the
renovation project. The client will have full access to available trade discounts. In consideration of these services, the client
agrees to a 10% procurement fee, separate from material costs.
Custom, made-to-order, or special-order materials are non-cancelable and non-refundable once ordered.
Appliances
Appliances shall be purchased directly by the Client. Hello Chapter NJ LLC may advise on selection and assist with
coordination but does not purchase, install, warrant, or service appliances.
